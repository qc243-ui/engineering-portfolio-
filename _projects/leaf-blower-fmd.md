# Leaf Blower — Functional Mechanical Dissection (FMD)

**Course:** MAE 3230

## Project Overview

For our Functional Mechanical Dissection (FMD) project, our group will disassemble and analyze a leaf blower to understand its mechanical components, operating principles, and overall design. We will investigate how the individual components work together to generate airflow and examine the engineering decisions behind the system.

This page will be updated throughout the project with photographs, component analysis, and our findings.
